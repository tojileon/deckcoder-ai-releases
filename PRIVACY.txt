DeckCoderAI desktop beta — privacy notice
30 October 2026

DeckCoderAI is developed by Toji Leon. Contact toji@tojileon.com with
DeckCoderAI privacy in the subject for privacy questions.

Local files and settings
The app saves presentations, speaker notes, assets and version history on
your computer. It also stores preferences, recent presentation paths,
thumbnail caches, agent connection settings and recovery data in its local
application profile. Recovery data and history can contain earlier content.
Removing the app alone does not remove presentations or its profile.
Folders you place in iCloud, OneDrive or another sync service are governed
by that service's settings and policies.

AI tools
DeckCoderAI does not provide a hosted AI model. When you connect an external
agent or paste an authoring brief into one, that tool can receive the
content you share and the data its permitted tools read, including slides,
notes or images. The AI provider's terms and privacy policy apply. MCP
access can be closed in Agent access. Separate file and computer-control
tools have their own permissions. AI subscriptions and usage fees are not
included in the free desktop app.

Network connections
The app has no built-in analytics service or automatic crash-report upload.
Presentations may load websites, remote assets, YouTube or Vimeo content.
Those services receive network information such as your IP address and may
process content or cookies under their own policies. A presentation can
contain authored JavaScript that makes network requests. Local storage of
a deck does not make all of its content offline or private. Open content
you trust and check its external dependencies before presenting or sharing.

Sharing and support
Browser exports exclude speaker notes. Presentation snapshots include notes
and local assets; PDF and PowerPoint exports may include notes depending
on the export option. Inspect files before sharing. Support email is handled
through the mailbox provider; send only the information needed to diagnose
the problem, preferably a minimal example with private content removed.
Support correspondence is used to answer requests and investigate issues.
Ask the contact above about deletion of correspondence; applicable legal
retention requirements may limit deletion.

Deletion and updates
You control your deck files and exported copies. The application profile is
normally under ~/Library/Application Support/DeckCoderAI on macOS and
%APPDATA%/DeckCoderAI on Windows. An upgraded installation may retain an
older DeckCoder AI or HTML Presenter profile. Close the app and back up
needed content before deleting a profile, including recovery data. Deleting
local files does not remove copies already shared or held by cloud services.
This beta uses manual downloads for updates. Website and download-host
data handling must be described on the distribution website separately.
